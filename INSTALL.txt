ARTIFACT WIDGETS — pin your Claude artifacts to your Mac desktop as live widgets
==============================================================================

Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

INSTALL
1. Drag "Artifact Widgets" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS will block it, because
   the app isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer:
     - Click "Done" (or "OK") on the warning.
     - Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway" next to "Artifact Widgets". Confirm with your password.
   You only do this once.

FIRST RUN
3. A "Sign in to Claude" window opens. Sign in with your claude.ai account.
   (If Google sign-in is blocked, use the email code option.)
   The window closes by itself when you're in.
4. Click the grid icon in the menu bar → New Widget → From Your Account,
   and pick an artifact. Add as many widgets as you like.

USING WIDGETS
- Hover a widget: a small pill appears in its top-right corner.
    Arrows icon: drag to move (or hold ⌘ and drag anywhere on the widget).
    ≡ button: switch artifact, reload, auto-refresh, zoom, remove.
- Drag a widget's edge to resize it.
- Widgets sit on your desktop: above desktop icons, below your other windows.
  Menu bar → "Keep Widgets Above Windows" floats them on top instead.
- Menu bar → "Launch at Login" brings them back after a restart.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Unofficial and not made by Anthropic. It shows claude.ai pages in a
  built-in browser and hides claude.ai's toolbars. If claude.ai changes
  its pages, parts of this may stop working until the app is updated.
- Your sign-in and settings stay on your Mac:
  ~/Library/Application Support/ArtifactWidgets/
- To uninstall: quit it from the menu bar, delete the app, and delete the
  folder above.
